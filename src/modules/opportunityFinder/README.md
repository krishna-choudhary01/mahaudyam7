# OpportunityFinder Module

## Overview
`OpportunityFinder` helps rural entrepreneurs identify viable micro-enterprises and agro-processing / service business opportunities tailored to their taluka/district resources in Maharashtra.

## Responsibilities
- Rule-based opportunity matching based on local raw materials, initial capital, and skill level.
- Offline storage of opportunity profiles, investment ranges, and viability indicators.
- Localized recommendations (e.g., cold storage, jaggery production, goat farming, solar service).

## Phase 1 Status
Architecture defined. Detailed features and business logic will be implemented in subsequent phases per the roadmap.
